About the Instructor

The workshop is taught from the perspective that Arabic NLP is both a technical practice and a critical humanities method.

اللغة والمنهج والقراءة

Joudy Sido Bozan

Joudy Sido Bozan works at the intersection of Arabic textual studies, digital methods, and natural language processing. Her teaching emphasizes careful first steps into computational work: learning how tools operate, how Arabic text becomes data, and how scholarly interpretation remains essential when working with corpora and models.

This workshop is designed especially for participants who are new to programming environments. It combines practical notebook exercises with questions about Arabic script, morphology, dialect representation, digitization, corpus construction, and the responsible interpretation of model outputs.

Teaching Approach

Beginner-first

The workshop assumes no prior experience with Terminal, Python, JupyterLab, or digital methods.

Arabic-aware

Examples center Arabic script, Unicode, diacritics, morphology, dialects, and corpus context.

Critically grounded

Participants learn to treat NLP outputs as evidence that needs context, not as automatic answers.
