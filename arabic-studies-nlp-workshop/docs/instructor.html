About the Instructor

The workshop is taught from the perspective that Arabic NLP is both a technical practice and a critical humanities method.

Joudy Sido Bozan

Joudy Sido Bozan is a Research Software Developer at Freie Universität Berlin, specializing in the representation of Arabic language and knowledge systems within digital humanities infrastructures. Her work centers on developing multilingual research environments, particularly through the bilingual (Arabic/English) Living Handbook kalimat, where she contributes to editorial workflows, interface design, and sustainable data practices using the Open Encyclopedia System (OES). She is especially interested in the challenges of modeling, processing, and publishing Arabic script in digital contexts, advocating for more inclusive and technically robust approaches to non-Latin data. Alongside her institutional work, she contributes to the Berlin University Alliance project “Closing the Gap in Non-Latin Script Data II,” developing AI-driven tools to evaluate research sustainability. With a background in Arabic Studies and Contemporary Arabic Studies, her interdisciplinary work bridges language, technology, and cultural analysis in the Arab world.

Teaching Approach

Beginner-first

The workshop assumes no prior experience with Google Colab, Python, notebooks, or digital methods.

Arabic-aware

Examples center Arabic script, Unicode, diacritics, morphology, dialects, and corpus context.

Critically grounded

Participants learn to treat NLP outputs as evidence that needs context, not as automatic answers.
