Workshop route

Six-Hour Schedule

The workshop moves from a shared introduction to NLP boundaries, Arabic NLP, hands-on coding, critical reflection on bias, and evaluation.

Agenda

Time Segment Focus
10:00-10:30 Introduction Workshop aims, participant orientation, and shared starting points
10:30-11:00 What is (NOT) NLP Distinguish NLP from adjacent methods such as OCR, HTR, computer vision, and general data work
11:00-12:00 Arabic NLP Arabic script, preprocessing, tokenization, linguistic variation, and method limits
12:00-13:00 Lunch Break Break
13:00-15:00 Let's code! Hands-on Colab work with Arabic text, corpus exploration, and model demonstrations
15:00-15:30 Beyond Model Bias Critical reflection on bias, corpus construction, representation, and interpretive responsibility
15:30-16:00 Evaluation & outlook Workshop evaluation, open questions, next steps, and continued learning

Session Route

Introduction

Establish the workshop questions, shared vocabulary, and practical orientation.

What is (NOT) NLP

Classify literary and manuscript studies cases as NLP, not NLP, or mixed.

Arabic NLP

Connect Arabic writing, morphology, variation, and preprocessing to computational text analysis.

Let's code!

Work through the shared Colab notebook, from basic text inspection to corpus and model exercises.

Beyond Model Bias

Discuss what bias means in relation to corpora, digitization, language varieties, and scholarly claims.

Evaluation & outlook

Evaluate the workshop and identify paths for further practice and research.

Pacing and Flexibility

If time is short

We will keep the conceptual framing and final evaluation intact, then shorten optional coding extensions rather than rushing the interpretation work.

If time remains

We can extend the coding session with additional corpus searches, model examples, or discussion of participant research questions.

Essential orientation

The introduction and What is (NOT) NLP sections establish the method vocabulary used throughout the hands-on work.

For the live workshop, keep this schedule open next to the shared Google Colab notebook.
