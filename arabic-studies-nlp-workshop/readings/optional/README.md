# Optional Readings

**Use after:** the workshop  
**Purpose:** continue from introductory practice toward independent study

These readings are for participants who want to continue after the workshop.

| Theme | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| AraBERT and Arabic models | Model purpose, training data, evaluation tasks, and release notes |
| Arabic NLP surveys | Morphology, dialects, resources, and recurring technical challenges |
| Dialect representation | Which dialects are modeled, omitted, or grouped together |
| OCR and digitized heritage | Error patterns, transcription choices, and corpus reliability |
| Language and power | How infrastructure shapes what becomes analyzable |
| Reproducible research | How to document data, code, preprocessing, and interpretation |

## Reading Strategy

1. First, identify the corpus or dataset being discussed.
2. Then, identify the technical method.
3. Finally, ask what social, historical, or linguistic assumptions shape the result.

> **Academic caution:** do not evaluate an NLP method only by its technical performance. Ask whether the data and task fit the research question.
