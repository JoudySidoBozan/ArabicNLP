Glossary

Term Meaning
AlgorithmA set of instructions that a computer follows to complete a task.
Code cellA notebook block that contains runnable Python code.
Markdown cellA notebook block that contains explanation, headings, or notes.
PythonThe programming language used in the workshop examples.
Jupyter notebookAn interactive document that combines explanation, code, and output.
TokenizationBreaking text into words or smaller units.
NormalizationConverting text to a standard form for processing.
MorphologyThe study of word structure and how words form.
CorpusA structured collection of texts used for analysis.
ConcordanceA list of places where a word appears together with nearby context.
ModelA computational system trained to identify patterns or produce predictions.
DialectA regional or social variety of a language.
DiglossiaThe coexistence of high and low varieties of Arabic in different contexts.
OCROptical character recognition, a process that converts images of text into machine-readable text.
BiasA systematic limitation or imbalance in data, tools, or interpretation.

Suggested Readings

Resource Why it matters
CAMeL Tools documentation Arabic-aware NLP tools used in the workshop.
OpenITI project Large-scale corpus context for Arabic and Islamicate texts.
KITAB project Corpus research, text reuse, and Arabic textual traditions.
Humanities-oriented Arabic NLP surveys Morphology, dialects, script, resources, and recurring technical challenges.
Critical discussions of OCR and corpus bias Digitization quality, representativeness, and responsible interpretation.
Reading strategy: first identify the corpus or dataset, then the technical method, then the social, historical, or linguistic assumptions behind the result.

Use the glossary during the workshop whenever a term feels unfamiliar.
