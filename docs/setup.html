Before the workshop

Prepare Your Laptop

You do not need to download the repository or install Python. The hands-on work will happen in Google Colab.

Before you arrive: bring a laptop, use or create a Gmail/Google account, and make sure the account has about 6 GB of free Google Drive storage.

Colab Readiness Checklist

Step Action Success marker
1 Bring your laptop You can connect to the workshop Wi-Fi and open a browser
2 Use an existing Gmail/Google account or create one You can sign in at accounts.google.com
3 Check Google Drive storage About 6 GB is available for Colab files and runtime data
4 Open the workshop Colab notebook The notebook opens in your browser

Colab Troubleshooting

Symptom First thing to check
You cannot open the notebook Check that you are signed in to a Google account and try Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari.
Google Drive says storage is full Free space in Drive or use another Google account with about 6 GB available.
A cell asks for permission Read the prompt, then approve access only when it is needed for your own notebook and Drive files.
The runtime disconnects Reconnect from the Colab toolbar and rerun earlier cells if needed.
A notebook cell runs for a long time It may be installing CAMeL Tools or downloading model data. Ask before interrupting it.

If Technology Fails

Online notebook problems are common in first digital methods workshops. The workshop can continue even if not every account or runtime works perfectly.

Problem Workshop response
Many Colab sessions fail Follow from the shared screen and focus on concepts, outputs, and interpretation.
Some accounts cannot save a copy Pair with someone whose Colab copy works, or continue conceptually until the next pause.
Internet is slow Keep the instructor's shared screen as the reference and rerun cells when the connection improves.
Runtime setup is slow Discuss the expected output while package setup or downloads continue in the background.
Remember: the core goal is to understand the workflow and its limits, not to prove that every account can run every cell at the same speed.
